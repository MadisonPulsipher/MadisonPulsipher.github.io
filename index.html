Madison Pulsipher

Anesthesiologist

Hi! My name is Madison Pulsipher, and I am currently a Division I student-athlete at the University of California, Riverside, where I play on the women's volleyball team and major in Global and Community Health. I have always been passionate about helping others, challenging myself, and making a positive impact on people's lives. My long-term career goal is to become an anesthesiologist, combining my interest in medicine with my desire to provide compassionate patient care. My passion for anesthesiology began when I underwent surgery at a young age. I remember feeling nervous before my procedure, but the anesthesiologist reassured me and helped me feel safe. That experience left a lasting impression on me and inspired me to pursue a career where I could provide that same comfort to others. I am especially interested in learning how anesthesia works, how medications affect the human body, and how anesthesiologists play such an important role in patient safety.

Along with my education, I have gained valuable professional experience in healthcare, customer service, and leadership. I previously worked as a back-office assistant at Springs Dental, where I gained firsthand experience in a healthcare environment and learned the importance of patient communication, professionalism, and attention to detail. I also worked as a barista at Cactus Nutrition, which helped me strengthen my communication, teamwork, and customer service skills. Additionally, I have coached young volleyball players at Valley Volleyball Academy, where I developed leadership skills and learned how to motivate and encourage others. One of my most meaningful experiences was spending time at my family's dental clinic in Tonga, where I gained a greater appreciation for accessible healthcare and the importance of serving communities. Each of these experiences has helped shape my work ethic and strengthened my passion for pursuing a career in medicine.

Being a Division I volleyball player has also played a significant role in preparing me for my future career. Balancing academics, athletics, and professional responsibilities has taught me discipline, time management, perseverance, and the ability to remain calm under pressure. I believe these qualities will be essential in anesthesiology, where making important decisions, communicating effectively, and working as part of a medical team are necessary for providing excellent patient care. Outside of school and volleyball, I enjoy spending time with family and friends, traveling, staying active, and experiencing new things. Looking toward the future, I hope to attend medical school and specialize in anesthesiology, where I can make a meaningful difference in patients' lives. My ultimate goal is to become a compassionate and knowledgeable physician who helps patients feel safe, comfortable, and supported throughout their medical experiences. I am excited to continue learning, growing, and working toward a career that allows me to combine my passion for science with my commitment to helping others.
